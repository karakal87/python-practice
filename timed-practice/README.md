# Timed practice — HackerRank / LeetCode

This is the second half of the daily 30 minutes: one problem, timed, AI off,
on the actual platform (not in this repo). This folder just tracks what you
did and how it went, since neither site's history follows you here.

## The routine

1. Pick the next problem from the list below (work top to bottom — it's
   roughly ordered to match what Phase A is covering week by week).
2. Set a timer: **20 minutes** for an Easy problem.
3. Solve it on the platform, unaided.
4. Whatever happens — solved, partially solved, stuck — log it in
   `log.md` before you move on. The log is the point as much as the solving.
5. If you didn't finish, look at the editorial/solution afterwards, understand
   it, and note in the log what you missed. Don't re-attempt same-day; come
   back to a similar problem in a week and see if it's faster.

## Why 20 minutes and not "until solved"

Real screening tests are timed. Training yourself to recognise "I'm not
going to get this in time, let me write what I have and move on" is itself
an interview skill. Open-ended struggling doesn't build that.
